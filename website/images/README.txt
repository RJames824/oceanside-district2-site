PHOTOS GO HERE
==============

Save your photos into THIS folder using these exact file names and they
appear on the site automatically (no code editing needed). Until a file
is added, that spot shows a clean labeled placeholder.

WHICH PHOTO GOES WHERE  (based on the photos you sent)
------------------------------------------------------
  ricky-hero.jpg    -> TOP of the page, large portrait.
                       Recommended: your professional suit headshot,
                       OR the indoor white-tee photo (smiling, plant behind).
                       Best shape: portrait (taller than wide).

  ricky-about.jpg   -> "About Ricky" section.
                       Recommended: the family photo by the olive tree with
                       holiday ornaments (you, your wife, and your son),
                       or the Christmas-lights tunnel family photo.

  gallery-1.jpg     -> "On the River Trail" -> the handcycle photo towing
                       your son in the bike trailer.
  gallery-2.jpg     -> "Access for everyone" -> the beach photo on the
                       access mat with your son holding the wheel.
  gallery-3.jpg     -> "Our coast" -> the sportfishing boat photo with the fish.
  gallery-4.jpg     -> "Our neighborhoods" -> the kids in front of the
                       Oceanside Fire Department engine.

(All of these are just suggestions -- swap in whatever you like, as long as
the file names match.)

HOW TO ADD THEM
---------------
1. Pick the photo, rename it to the name above (e.g. ricky-hero.jpg).
2. Drag it into this images folder.
3. Refresh the page in your browser.

TIPS
----
- JPG or PNG both fine. If a photo is a .png or .heic, either convert it to
  .jpg, or rename and update the matching src= line in index.html.
- Keep each file under ~500 KB so the page loads fast (resize/compress free
  at squoosh.app). Phone photos are often 3-5 MB -- worth shrinking.
- Portrait-orientation photos fit the frames best.
